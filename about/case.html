Notes

iCloud

I came to software sideways.

Growing up I trained myself to see. Really see. How light pulls through curtain fabric, how shadow gives a sketch weight. Software arrived through aesthetics, through the kind of attention that makes you want to stop on something and understand it completely.

I think about incentive structures the way other people think about colour. The architecture of a thing tells you what it values. I try to build tools that belong to their users and leave scaffolding for whoever comes after me.

An endometriosis screener disguised as combat mechanics, because people do not fill out forms about their pain but they do finish games. A Baldur’s Gate 3 expansion at actual studio scale. Every project starts the same way: a question I cannot put down.
